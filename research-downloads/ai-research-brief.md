# Before an investment

Establish the source-verified catalyst and what is already priced in.

Complete issuer, original-filing, valuation and scenario review.

Record entry conditions, thesis invalidation and the intended holding horizon.

Verify the actual broker instrument, fresh quote, spread, financing and full holding-period costs.

Obtain independent clearance and measured sizing/risk checks. Research conviction alone is insufficient.

AURORA uses a six-hour minimum ordinary hold, with earlier exits for evidenced thesis changes or protective risk conditions. No fast-trading instruction is created by a technical indicator.
